## Selected work

### 🔬 [Atlas — Autonomous ML Research Platform](https://github.com/ToshitSai/atlas)
A conversational research assistant that discovers datasets, runs real analysis and model experiments, and turns findings into reports. Built to keep measured results grounded in actual execution.

`React 19` · `FastAPI` · `scikit-learn` · `PostgreSQL` · `Hugging Face`

### 🤖 [CourseForge AI](https://github.com/ToshitSai/courseforge-ai)
Turns a topic into structured learning material, quizzes, and curated video resources.

`React` · `Gemini API` · `YouTube API`

`Next.js` · `Claude API` · `Puppeteer`
